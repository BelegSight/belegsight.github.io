Privacy Policy & GDPR Disclosure

Effective Date: 2026-11-16

1. Overview & Zero-Data-Collection Architecture

BelegSight ("we", "our", or "Vendor") provides the BelegSight software application for Atlassian Jira Cloud and Jira Service Management exclusively on the Atlassian Forge (Runs on Atlassian) platform.

We do not collect, transmit, store, or have access to your Jira issues, invoice attachments, supplier IBANs, tax identifiers, or personal data on any Vendor-controlled server.

2. Where Invoice Data Is Processed

  • In-Platform Execution Only: All invoice parsing, validation, and export operations execute strictly within your Atlassian Cloud environment using Atlassian Forge functions with zero external network egress.
  • Tenant-Scoped Metadata: Admin settings (Custom Field IDs, DATEV account defaults, and company SEPA debtor settings) are stored solely in Atlassian's tenant-isolated Forge App Storage (storage:app). Per-issue validation summaries are stored solely inside your Jira Cloud tenant as Jira Issue Entity Properties (belegsight.intake.v1) and mapped Jira Custom Fields.
  • Uninstallation & Deletion: Uninstalling BelegSight or deleting the underlying Jira issues removes the associated Jira issue properties within your Atlassian Cloud environment in accordance with Atlassian's platform lifecycle.

3. Marketplace Billing & Support Communications

  • Atlassian Marketplace Licensing: Commercial licensing, billing, and tax invoicing are processed by Atlassian Pty Ltd as Merchant of Record under Atlassian's Privacy Policy. Atlassian provides Marketplace Partners with standard B2B license transaction metadata (such as company/workspace name, tier, and technical/billing contact if shared under Atlassian Marketplace terms) solely for license administration.
  • Support Inquiries: If you email belegsight@gmail.com or open an issue on our public documentation tracker, we process your contact details and message solely to resolve your support request. Do not attach unredacted production invoices containing live personal data or live bank credentials to support tickets.
  • Static Documentation Website: This documentation website (belegsight.github.io) is hosted statically on GitHub Pages with zero advertising cookies, zero analytics pixels, and zero behavioral tracking scripts.

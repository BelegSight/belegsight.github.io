BelegSight Documentation & Administrator Guide

Version: 1.0 • Platform: Atlassian Forge (Runs on Atlassian, Node.js 22 runtime) • Products: Jira Cloud & Jira Service Management (JSM)

1. Installation & Zero-Config Startup

  1. Install BelegSight from the Atlassian Marketplace into your Jira Cloud / Jira Service Management site (choose Standard or Advanced Edition; both include a free Atlassian Marketplace evaluation period).
  2. Open any Jira or JSM issue containing an attached .xml (XRechnung / Peppol / EN 16931) or hybrid .pdf (ZUGFeRD 2.3 / Factur-X 1.07) invoice.
  3. Click the BelegSight E-Invoice (EN 16931) Issue Panel button to view the rendered invoice in DE, EN, or FR alongside the EN 16931 validation log and PDF-vs-XML Fraud Guard status.

2. Auto-Provisioning Jira Custom Fields for JSM Approvals

To route invoices automatically through Jira Service Management approval gates (e.g., requiring CFO sign-off above EUR 5,000.00 while blocking tampered PDFs):

  1. Navigate to Jira Administration (⚙️) → Apps → BelegSight E-Invoice Settings.
  2. Click 1-Click Auto-Create Standard Fields (or map your existing Jira Custom Fields):
    • BelegSight Gross Total (Number — BT-112)
    • BelegSight VAT Amount (Number — BT-110)
    • BelegSight Currency (Text — BT-5)
    • BelegSight Supplier IBAN (Text — BT-84)
    • BelegSight Invoice Number (Text — BT-1)
    • BelegSight Due Date (Date — BT-9)
    • BelegSight Leitweg-ID / Buyer Ref (Text — BT-10)
    • BelegSight Audit Verdict (Text — VALID, DISCREPANCY_BLOCKED, or INVALID)
  3. Add the mapped fields to your JSM Project Screen if required by your Jira field configuration.
  4. Whenever a supplier emails or uploads an invoice attachment, BelegSight's idempotent avi:jira:created:attachment trigger computes a deterministic SHA-256 digest, validates the invoice, stores the summary in Jira Issue Property belegsight.intake.v1, and updates your mapped Custom Fields automatically.

3. Recommended JQL & JSM Workflow Conditions

Because BelegSight writes a structured Jira Issue Entity Property (belegsight.intake.v1) in addition to Custom Fields, you can block payment transitions whenever an EN 16931 error or PDF-vs-XML discrepancy exists:

"BelegSight Audit Verdict" = "VALID" AND "BelegSight Gross Total" > 5000

4. Exporting Approved Invoices to DATEV EXTF & SEPA pain.001 (Advanced Edition)

  1. Open your Jira / JSM project sidebar and select BelegSight DATEV & SEPA Export.
  2. BelegSight lists all project issues in your configured Approved statuses (default: Approved, Freigegeben, Zur Zahlung freigegeben, Done).
  3. DATEV EXTF Buchungsstapel: Enter your Beraternummer, Mandantennummer, and chart of accounts (SKR03 or SKR04), then click Download EXTF_Buchungsstapel.csv. Multi-rate invoices (19% + 7%) are split into separate booking rows with matching BU-Schlüssel (9 for 19%, 8 for 7%, 94 for § 13b Reverse Charge).
  4. SEPA Credit Transfer (pain.001): Enter your company Debtor Name, Debtor IBAN, Execution Date, and schema version (pain.001.001.09 current Deutsche Kreditwirtschaft / EPC standard, or pain.001.001.03 legacy), then click Download SEPA pain.001 XML. Any ticket with DISCREPANCY_BLOCKED, INVALID, credit-note type 381, or non-EUR currency is automatically excluded and listed in the skip log.
